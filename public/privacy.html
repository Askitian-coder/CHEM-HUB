Privacy Notice

Effective 14 November 2026

ChemHub by Askitian is a chemistry study aid for secondary-school students. This notice explains the information used by the site and how to ask questions or request account-data changes. It is an operational notice, not legal advice.

Information used

  • Account details: your email address and authentication details are sent to Supabase Authentication when you sign up, sign in, or request an email confirmation. ChemHub does not keep a separate copy of your raw password.
  • Consent confirmation: when an account is created, the site submits the privacy-notice version, confirmation flag, and timestamp as account metadata. The confirmation is self-attested; we do not independently verify age or guardian identity.
  • Study progress: if signed in, completed-topic status and study activity dates are stored in Supabase so the progress dashboard can be synchronized to your account.
  • Authentication session: the Supabase client stores the sign-in session in browser storage so you remain signed in. Signing out clears the active session.
  • Hosting requests: GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process ordinary technical request information such as IP address and browser details under its own terms.

Why and with whom

Account information is used to authenticate you, send confirmation messages, and save the study progress you request. Supabase provides authentication and database hosting. ChemHub does not use advertising or analytics tags; Google Tag Manager has been removed. An optional, separately configured administrator report may send aggregate account totals to the site maintainer through an email provider. It contains counts, not account email addresses.

Children and guardian permission

If you are under 18, ask a parent or guardian to review this notice and the Terms and agree before you create an account. The sign-up checkbox records a self-attestation only; it is not an age check or verified guardian-consent process. Do not enter another person’s information.

Retention and requests

Account and progress data remain in the connected Supabase project until the account is deleted or a deletion request is processed. The site currently has no self-service account-deletion control. To ask for access, correction, or deletion of your ChemHub account data, contact askitian@gmail.com from the account email address. Deletion may take time to propagate through provider backups.

Changes and contact

We will update this page when the site’s data practices change. Questions about this notice can be sent to askitian@gmail.com.

ChemHub is a study aid, not an official WAEC resource or affiliated with any school. Check answers with your teacher.
